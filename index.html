How to ride

  • ✋Press and hold the screen to pedal. Let go and the bike brakes to a stop.
  • ↔Drag left or right while holding to steer. Keyboard: hold ↑ or W to ride, ← → to steer.
  • ⚡Grab blue energy and ride close behind rivals to fill boost. Tap the boost button to fire it.
  • GOBoost right on GO for a perfect start. Too early and you stall.
  • ↑Hit a ramp, then tap FLIP, FRONT, SPIN or ROLL in the air. Chain several for a combo. Red MEGA RAMPS give huge air. The buttons turn orange when time is short. Land mid-trick and you crash.
  • !Dodge danfo buses, keke and barriers. Cones slow you, oil makes you slide.
  • IDYour rider name is printed on your jacket. Change it any time in the Garage or Settings.
  • BOSSEach chapter ends with a boss rider. Later chapters are faster and tougher. Collect stars to open the next chapter.
  • ★Finish a race, then tap Continue for a brand-new scene. Top 3 earns stars and bonus coins.
