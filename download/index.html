Install once. New tracks, bikes and fixes arrive by themselves every time you open the app. No need to download again.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns about the file, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open Bike RUSH and tap Allow when it asks about notifications, so you hear when new tracks are added. The first launch needs internet; after that it also works offline.

Already have an older Bike RUSH installed? Install this over it. Your coins, bikes and progress carry over.

On iPhone or iPad

Bike RUSH installs straight from Safari onto your Home Screen. No App Store needed, and updates arrive by themselves.

  1. Open sommyart-bike-rush.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (or top) of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Bike RUSH from your Home Screen. It runs full screen like an app.
  5. For new-track alerts, go to Settings in the game and turn on New track alerts, then tap Allow. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer.
